About

I’m drawn to the space where ideas become stories.

I’ve always been interested in the space where knowledge meets people, and where information becomes something people can understand and connect with.

I enjoy taking ideas, research and everyday experiences and finding a way to make them clear, thoughtful and human.

My writing explores health, research, lifestyle and everyday experiences. I also work as a ghostwriter, helping people turn their ideas into words that still sound like them.
