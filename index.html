About

I have always been interested in the stories behind the facts.

I'm Humaida, a writer and researcher with a background in microbiology and public health. I enjoy exploring ideas, research and everyday experiences, then finding simple ways to make them clear and human.

My writing moves across health, research, lifestyle and long-form content. I also ghostwrite, helping people turn their ideas into words that still sound like them.

Good writing doesn't simply give information. It gives the reader a way to see it.
