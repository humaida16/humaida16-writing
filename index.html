A little about me

I have always been interested in the stories behind the facts.

Hi, I'm Humaida. I'm a writer and researcher with a background in microbiology and public health, and I have always been drawn to the space where knowledge meets people.

I enjoy taking ideas, research and conversations that may feel complicated and finding a way to make them clear, thoughtful and human.

My writing moves across health, research, lifestyle and long-form content. I also work behind the scenes as a ghostwriter, helping people turn their ideas into words that sound natural and true to them.
