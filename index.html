About me

I’m curious about what sits beneath the surface.

I’m Humaida, a writer and researcher with a background in microbiology and public health. I’m drawn to the stories behind the facts, the questions that make us look twice, and the everyday experiences that often say more than we realise.

I enjoy taking research, ideas and observations and turning them into writing that feels clear, thoughtful and human.

My work explores health, research, lifestyle and everyday life, and I also work as a ghostwriter, helping people shape their ideas into words that still feel like their own.
